    Approach to neonatal haemodynamic evaluation
    
    
      
      
    1. History — antenatal, perinatal, current illness and trajectory.
      2. 
      
    2. Examination & vitals — pre/post-ductal saturations, blood pressure, perfusion, NIRS, current medications; consider situs and possible shunts.
      3. 
      
    3. Saturations & pressures by vessel and chamber — reason through shunt direction and magnitude.
      4. 
      
    4. Investigations — Hb, blood gas, electrolytes, NT-proBNP, chest X-ray, ECG.
      5. 
      
    5. TnECHO findings — systolic/diastolic function, hypertrophy, outflow obstruction, valves, shunts, PA pressure & PVR, venous returns, line tips, effusions.
      6. 
      
    6. Management plan — cardiorespiratory support, electrolytes, preload/filling, inotropes, pulmonary and systemic vasodilation, adrenal support, follow-up imaging.
      7. 
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    Sequence at a glance
    
    
      
      
    1. Parasternal long axis
    2. Parasternal short axis
    3. Apical views (4C, 3C, 2C, RV 3C, TDI)
      4.

    About
    
    
    This app is a bedside checklist built on the TnECHO Echocardiography Protocol published by NeoCardio Lab (Montreal Children's Hospital, Dr G. Altit). Step wording is paraphrased; refer to the original protocol and its PDF for full detail and images.
    
    
    Open the source protocol ↗
    
    
    The Training tab uses annotated figures from McNamara PJ et al., Guidelines and Recommendations for Targeted Neonatal Echocardiography and Cardiac Point-of-Care Ultrasound in the NICU, J Am Soc Echocardiogr 2024;37:171–215 (© 2023 American Society of Echocardiography, published by Elsevier Inc.), reproduced with permission; and from El-Khuffash A (ed), Neonatologist Performed Echocardiography Teaching Manual, 2019 edition, The Rotunda Hospital, Dublin, reproduced with permission. Teaching text is adapted.
    
    
    Note: item 2e (PSAX) was not available in the source page at the time of building and is shown as “refer to source”.
    
    
    Clips, measurements & privacy
    
    
    Clips, photos and studies are stored only in this browser on this device (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded. "Read values" uses on-device text recognition (Tesseract.js) to read the echo machine's own measurement panel; caliper measurements need a scale set from the image's depth/velocity/time markers. Always check values before use. Redact names and IC numbers before sharing a PDF. Clearing browser data deletes the logbook — export PDFs/CSV regularly.
    
    
    Disclaimer
    
    
    Educational and workflow aid only. Not a medical device. Calculations must be verified; interpretation remains the responsibility of the performing clinician and must follow local unit guidance. No data leaves the device.
